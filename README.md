# Unilever Financial Performance Dashboard (FY2024)

An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing **Unilever PLC's FY2024 financial performance**, built from the company's publicly reported annual results. The dashboard tracks the metrics an analyst uses to judge the financial health and quality of a business — profitability, earnings growth, capital efficiency and cash generation — and breaks performance down by business group and geography.

## 📊 What it covers
- **Headline P&L:** Turnover, Operating Margin %, Underlying EPS YoY Growth %
- **Capital efficiency & cash:** Return on Invested Capital (ROIC %) and Free Cash Flow — the metrics that show whether growth is actually creating value
- **Segment view:** Turnover split across Unilever's business groups
- **Geographic view:** Turnover split across regions
- **Driver analysis:** a decomposition tree to break down what's moving the headline numbers

## 🛠️ Built with
- **Power BI** — data modelling across multiple tables, custom DAX measures
- **Python visual** integrated within the report
- Decomposition tree, KPI cards, and segment/geography breakdowns

## 📁 Data
All figures are drawn from Unilever's **publicly published FY2024 annual report**. No proprietary or confidential data is used.

## 💡 Why I built it
To practise reading a real listed company the way a financial or deal analyst would — not just *what* the numbers are, but *what they say about the business*: where growth is concentrated, whether margins are holding, and whether the company is converting profit into cash and returns above its cost of capital.
